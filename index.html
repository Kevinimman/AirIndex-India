INDEX INTERPRETATION

Understanding the Index

With a base period of 2024 = 100, an index level of 127.84 represents a higher aggregate airfare price level compared with the selected base period.

The index is designed to capture movements in comparable airfare observations instead of simply reporting the cheapest ticket available.

STATISTICAL METHODOLOGY

From Flight Search to Airfare Index

01

Route Selection

Select representative domestic city-pairs using traffic and coverage considerations.

02

Fare Collection

Collect comparable observations across airlines and OTAs.

03

Cleaning

Detect duplicate, missing, anomalous and unavailable fares.

04

Normalisation

Standardise fare components, route definitions and timing.

05

Aggregation

Apply representative weights to calculate index values.

CONCEPTUAL INDEX FORMULA
Airfare Index = Σ (Route Price Relative × Route Weight)
